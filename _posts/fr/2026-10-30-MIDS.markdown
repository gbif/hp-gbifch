# Public review of the Minimum Information about a Digital Specimen (MIDS) standard

The MIDS Task Group is pleased to announce that the public review of the Minimum Information about a Digital Specimen (MIDS) standard is now open.

The review begins on 5 October 2026 and will remain open until 4 December 2026.

MIDS is a new standard, and this is its first public review.

## How to participate

The public review is open to everyone, and we encourage all community members to participate.
